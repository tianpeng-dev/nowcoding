# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want NowCoding to be a practical, respectful project for developers who care
about local-first usage collection, privacy, and public activity surfaces.

Participants are expected to be direct, constructive, and considerate. Debate
technical decisions rigorously without attacking people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or secrets.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Submitting malicious code, fake telemetry, or intentionally misleading
  security reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation
for behavior that undermines the project. Security-sensitive reports should use
the process in `SECURITY.md`.
